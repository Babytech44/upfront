How it works

One small contract. It holds the position, collects its MUSD from Mezo, and hands the position back.

Think of it as rent paid in advance

A landlord's house earns rent every month, but the landlord needs money today. Someone pays the next three months of rent now, at a small discount, and collects the rent from the tenant until they have received what was agreed. The landlord never sells the house and owes nothing.

Here the house is your locked Bitcoin, the rent is the MUSD Mezo pays every week, and Upfront is the agent who holds the papers until the buyer has been paid. One difference: Mezo sets this rent each week. If it falls, the buyer waits longer, and you never owe a top-up.

When a seller would use it

You locked Bitcoin on Mezo for the long term and need MUSD this week, for a bill, a trade or a loan repayment. The lock cannot be opened early, and borrowing brings interest and a liquidation price. Selling a few weeks of the lock's income brings neither.

Example from Mezo mainnet on 12 Nov 2026: 1 BTC locked at 5x boost earned about 112 MUSD a week. Its owner could take about 1,188 MUSD today for roughly 12 weeks of that income.

When a buyer would use it

You hold MUSD and want it to earn. You pay less today than the position pays you over the coming weeks, and the difference is your return. In the seller's example, the buyer pays 1,200 MUSD and receives 1,349.

Upfront runs on Mezo testnet for now, so the amounts in the app have no real value.

OfferPick how much MUSD you want today. Your position moves into the contract. MUSD it already earned is paid to you first.
BuyA buyer pays. You receive the MUSD at once, less a 1% fee. The contract votes the position for Mezo's savings-vault gauge.
CollectEvery Thursday Mezo pays the position. Anyone can press Collect, and the MUSD goes to the buyer.
Back to youWhen the buyer has received the agreed amount, the position returns. You can also pay the rest early.
What the buyer should know
  • The weekly payout is set by Mezo and can change. If it falls, you are repaid more slowly.
  • Each offer has a time limit. After it, the position returns to the seller even if you have not received everything.
  • Upfront has not been audited.
What Upfront does not do
  • No pooled vault: every sale is one seller and one buyer.
  • No price oracle and no liquidation.
  • No admin keys, no upgrades. The 1% fee is fixed in the contract.
  • Permanent locks only, for now.
