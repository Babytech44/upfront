# Tester steps

This is the message sent to testers. The first tester followed an earlier version of it on 13 Nov 2026; the wording below includes what their feedback changed.

**Help test Upfront on Mezo testnet (about 10 minutes)**

Think of a landlord who is paid the next months of rent in advance: money now, and the house stays theirs. Upfront does that for BTC locked on Mezo. You sell the next weeks of its MUSD income for MUSD today, with no loan. It is my Mezo Buildathon entry. Testnet only: nothing here has real value.

App: https://upfront-black.vercel.app
Code: https://github.com/Babytech44/upfront

**Before you start:** use a test-only wallet account, not your main one. Your wallet will ask you to review and confirm each step; that is expected.

**A. Just look (1 minute, no wallet)**
1. Open the app. It shows an example position.
2. Move the weeks slider and watch "You get".

**B. Sell (about 8 minutes)**
1. Get test BTC at https://faucet.test.mezo.org
2. Go to https://testnet.mezo.org and open **Lock**. Enter 0.005 BTC, click **Max** for the lock period, turn ON **Auto max-lock**, and confirm. Do not vote with this lock.
3. Back in Upfront, click **Connect wallet**. Your position appears. If the top-right button shows a different account from the one you locked with, click it and choose **Switch account**.
4. Set the slider to 1 week and click **Offer it**. Confirm twice.
5. Find your offer under **My positions**. You can cancel it any time before someone buys it.

**C. Buy (optional, 3 minutes)**
1. On testnet.mezo.org click **Swap** and swap a little test BTC for at least 20 MUSD.
2. In Upfront, under **Buy**, click **Pay ... MUSD** on someone else's offer. Confirm twice.

**Then reply with:**
1. Your wallet address
2. Where did you get stuck or confused?
3. Was any word or number unclear?
4. Would you use this as a seller, a buyer, or neither, and why?

Good to know: if someone buys your offer, your locked position stays in the contract until the buyer has been paid (one to two weeks), then returns to you. You can also pay the rest early to get it back.
